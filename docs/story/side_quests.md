### 1. The Whistling Kettle
**Giver:** Bram, at his house  
**Hook:** Bram confides that his kettle, the one he brews his tea in every day, has started whistling an eerie tune at midnight. The tune sounds like something he once heard drifting up from the cemetery.  
**Steps:**
1. Search the cemetery's old dig sites and find a small, rusted handbell buried among the graves.
2. Bring the handbell to Bram's house. Bram recognises the tune in its ring.
3. Ring the handbell beside the kettle, once and softly, and hear the whistling fade.
**Twist:** Scratched into the kettle's base is an old sexton's mark, worn too smooth to read. Whoever owned it kept watch over the vale's very first grave, long before anyone in town can remember.
**Reward:** A hand-painted kettle that never whistles and always brews the perfect cup.

### 2. The Candlelit Journey
**Giver:** Ivy, at Ivy's house  
**Hook:** Ivy needs to bring a special candle to the chapel, but she's too scared to go out after dark. The candle is said to hold the light of the vale's first dawn, and the coming festival can't start without it.  
**Steps:**
1. Visit the mystic's shop, where an old candle recipe is kept.
2. Gather ingredients around town: beeswax from Ivy's house, old parchment from Sera, and a pinch of moon dust from the alchemy shop.
3. Make the candle, then carry it to the chapel and light it at the altar.
**Twist:** In the candle's glow, a faded mural appears behind the altar: a figure in black kneeling beside a great bell, under the ground.
**Reward:** A set of old storybooks for your shelf.

### 3. The Midnight Toll
**Giver:** Noll, at Noll's house  
**Note:** Noll is "her" (canon, matches content.ts).  
**Hook:** For three nights Noll has heard a deep bell toll at midnight, though there is no bell in town that sounds like it. After every toll, the graves in the cemetery stir.  
**Steps:**
1. Wait in the cemetery at midnight and listen. The toll comes from below the ground, not from the sky.
2. Find a tired ghost by the crypt stair who rises with every toll, whether it wants to or not.
3. Help the ghost settle its unfinished business so it can rest, even while the bell keeps calling.
**Twist:** The ghost says that every toll calls the dead up for one more night of mourning. It doesn't know who rings the bell, only that it's been ringing for a hundred years, and that the dead would rest if it ever fell silent.
**Reward:** The ghost's thanks, and a cold-fire candle stub that glows blue whenever the dead are near.
