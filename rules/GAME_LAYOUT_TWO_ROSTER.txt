SCALING  (do not invent another curve)
================================================================
Base numbers are pre-scale. Fight level L:
  HP     = round(base HP * (0.55 + L * 0.10) * bulk)
  Attack = round(base ATK * (0.70 + L * 0.06))
  Armor  = base AC + floor(L / 10)
  XP     = round(base XP * (0.80 + L * 0.08))
  Silver = round(base silver * (0.70 + L * 0.06))
bulk = 4.6 common (about four hits), 2 boss/remnant.
Remnant uses boss bulk and the same L. No helpers. Rare loot, not boss loot.

Boss helpers: count from YOUR level, not boss L.
  1–15:1  16–30:2  31–45:3  46–60:4  61–80:5  81+:6
Helper = ordinary family at round(boss L * 0.55), HP * 0.45.

Zone L of a tile:
  round(yourL * 0.35 + distanceBand + biome), min 1.
Distance from town gate (32,46): <8 +0, <16 +2, <26 +5, else +8.
Biome: vale +1, dry waste (x>48) +3, cinder (y>46) +3,
  winter (y<16) +4, scourge swamp (x 27–41 and y 23–39) +6.

Who uses what
  Wild / night: zone of tile. No floor. If fight L > you+4, spawn TWO.
  East bridge under lv 3: zone + 2.
  Dungeon: max(listed floor, zone of entrance). All floors + trash + boss share it.
  World boss: max(listed floor, zone of home tile).
  Remnant: same L as the boss it replaced.
  Grave / Hearth Stair: your L on entry, min 10. Ignore the map.
  Stalker: that fight L + 2, then HP * 1.45.
  Loot Goblin: your L, min 3.
A slain boss stays down two day-night cycles. Remnant stands in its place. Then the boss returns.
