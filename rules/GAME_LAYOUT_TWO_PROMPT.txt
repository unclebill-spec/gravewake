COMBAT — ASHFIELD
Trash: 1 move, no tell. Rare: 1 affix + 2–4 minions, 0.35s tint. Mini/remnant: SPAM+MID, 0.5s mark, no BIG.
[OWNER-APPROVED EXCEPTION 2026-09-30: MIMIC] Mimic only: a solo rare, no affix, no minions, one bite lunge on the 0.35s tint. Every other rare is still a pack.
Boss: spam / mid / BIG / helpers. BIG 0.8–1.2s decal. Walk out or eat it.
Affixes (one): Linked, Chained, Horde cap 6, Vortex, Mortar, Fast, Shielded.
No hard immunities. Never 0% from Slash/Whirl/Smite.

SCALE
HP = round(baseHP * (0.55+L*0.10) * bulk)
ATK = round(baseATK * (0.70+L*0.06))
AC  = baseAC + floor(L/10)
XP  = round(baseXP * (0.80+L*0.08))
Silver = round(baseSilver * (0.70+L*0.06))
bulk 4.6 common, 2 boss and remnant.

Zone L = round(you*0.35 + dist + biome), min 1.
Dist from gate 32,46: <8 +0, <16 +2, <26 +5, else +8.
Biome: vale +1, waste x>48 +3, cinder y>46 +3, winter y<16 +4, swamp x27-41 y23-39 +6.
Wild = zone of tile. If L > you+4, TWO.
Dungeon = max(floor, zone of entrance). World boss = max(floor, home tile).
Grave / Hearth = entry L, min 10.
[OWNER-APPROVED EXCEPTION 2026-10-01: MAP WRITER] Generated dungeon = the Dungeon rule: max(listed 14, zone of entrance 44,32), every floor, trash and guard. Rift zone = zone of the rift tile.
Stalker = L+2, HP*1.45. Goblin = your L, min 3.
Helpers from YOUR level: 1/2/3/4/5/6 at 1-15/16-30/31-45/46-60/61-80/81+.
Helper at round(bossL*0.55), HP*0.45.
Boss dead two day-night cycles. Remnant same L, no helpers, rare loot. Then boss returns.
